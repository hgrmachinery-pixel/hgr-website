## SEO and marketplace expansion boundaries

- [FUTURE] Automatic Mudah publishing/synchronisation: only after separate approval and current policy/capability review. Mudah and website listings remain separately manageable in V1.
- [DEFERRED] Exact UTM naming, listing identity, referral/campaign persistence and optional tracked-Mudah-link generation: decide during analytics/admin implementation without choosing technology here.
- [FUTURE] Brand/model/category/location and specific service pages: require genuine offerings/inventory, unique useful content and evidence from Search Console, Ads, keyword research and enquiries.
- [FUTURE] Advanced manual/offline sale attribution: website tracking alone does not prove an offline sale's source.
- [TO CONFIRM] Quantitative keyword data remains uncollected. Human-supplied qualitative research and approved themes are already incorporated; do not label them as entirely unresearched.

## Boundaries that must not drift

V1 still includes two admin accounts, secure no-code machinery management, simple search/filtering, price/status controls, actual photos, image processing/watermarks, optional YouTube video support, email enquiries, SEO foundations and core analytics/conversion measurement.

Secondary public pages remain in V1 under the confirmed models: opportunity-based rental, enquiry/quotation-based Machinery Services assistance, and request/supply-based lubricants. There may be no rental machines or lubricant products. Exact technical services, current availability, brands and coverage still need verification.

The planning estimate of 20–30 machines and 3–5 new listings per month is capacity context, not a published inventory or sales guarantee.

Review open V1 choices during Phase 2 and content approval. Review future features after launch issues are resolved and evidence supports expansion. Responsible reviewer and dates: [TO CONFIRM].
