# HGR Keyword Strategy

## Research status and authority

External Malaysian SEO and competitor research supplied by HGR has informed the general search landscape, terminology, competitor patterns and inventory structures. The human-approved qualitative strategy is recorded here; this is not a claim that Codex independently repeated the research.

Distinguish **research-informed keyword themes** from **quantitative keyword metrics still to collect**. Exact Google Keyword Planner metrics have NOT been collected. Monthly volume, CPC, Google Ads competition, keyword difficulty, traffic forecasts and ranking forecasts must not be invented. These themes are not ranked by volume. Missing data is uncollected, not zero.

Sources: [[00 - Project/HGR Project]], [[02 - Website Specification/Sitemap]], [[03 - Content/Company Information]], [[03 - Content/Website Copy]] and [[04 - SEO/SEO Strategy]]. Research provenance is the human-supplied external findings. Named competitor URLs, tool exports and quantitative evidence were not supplied in this update.

## Category and brand/model expansion

V1 focuses on Used Excavators. Each physical unit receives its own stable page, even if its model matches an earlier sold unit. Never reuse that sold URL for a new unit or model landing page.

[FUTURE] Brand/model/category pages need sufficient inventory, useful unique content and evidence from Search Console, keyword research, Ads or enquiries. Example /used-excavators/komatsu-pc200 is structural only, not an approved V1 page. Other machinery/product divisions require genuine offerings before targeting.

## Informational and location intent

No blog, multiple location pages or state/city rental pages are required for V1. First establish the national Used Excavators page, real-unit pages, Klang trust information and conversion paths.

[FUTURE] Useful informational subjects may include buying or inspecting a used excavator, understanding hours, choosing excavator size, undercarriage inspection and used versus reconditioned machinery. Publish only useful accurate content; do not generate generic articles merely to increase page count.

Future location pages need actual coverage, distinct value and evidence from Search Console, keyword research, Ads or enquiries. Avoid near-identical city pages.

## Intent and irrelevant traffic

Separate used-machine buying from rental, technical servicing, parts, operator training, employment, toys/games and unrelated equipment. A keyword's existence does not establish an HGR service.

For Ads, review candidate negatives against actual offerings/search-term evidence before exclusion. Do not automatically exclude rental, price-related queries or brands across every campaign. An Ads negative list is not an organic indexing rule.

## Remaining research and validation

1. Apply the approved qualitative themes and inventory-led priorities.
2. Verify machine/business facts and specific service/product claims.
3. Collect quantitative metrics later from appropriate authorised Google Ads/Keyword Planner or SEO data. Record source, date, country/language and definitions; forecasts require evidence and assumptions.
4. Review authorised historical account data if available. Post-launch Search Console analysis is not yet complete.
5. Maintain one primary page per intent cluster; do not create duplicate pages for close variants.
6. Review final titles, metadata and public copy against themes and confirmed facts; obtain HGR approval.
7. After launch, use Search Console, GA4, Ads search-term/conversion data, Mudah referral/campaign behaviour, customer enquiries and inventory performance to assess expansion.
8. Revise priorities based on observed relevance without turning estimates into guarantees.

Research-informed copy can use the approved themes now. Qualitative completion does not mean Keyword Planner or quantitative validation is complete.
