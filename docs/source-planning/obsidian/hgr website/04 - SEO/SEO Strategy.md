# HGR SEO Strategy

## Purpose and authority

Generate relevant machinery enquiries from organic search, with used excavator sales as the primary commercial focus. Success is useful traffic and enquiries, not an invented ranking or traffic promise.

Sources: [[00 - Project/HGR Project]], [[00 - Project/Business Requirements]], [[02 - Website Specification/Sitemap]], the page specifications and [[03 - Content/Company Information]]. This strategy incorporates the externally researched Malaysian search landscape and the human-approved SEO/Mudah direction supplied by HGR. Qualitative search terminology, competitor patterns and inventory structures are research-informed; no independent competitor audit or quantitative keyword-tool research is claimed by this update. Final public copy still requires factual review and approval.

## Research findings and competitive direction

The external research supplied by HGR identified large marketplaces, specialist heavy-equipment marketplaces, local machinery dealers and manufacturer used-equipment inventory systems in Malaysian search results.

Useful common information includes model, year, operating hours, condition, location, price, photographs and seller/contact details. Some systems also show serial numbers, categories, deeper specifications and financing/delivery/warranty/service claims. Their presence on competitors' sites does not establish HGR capability.

HGR should compete through real inventory and photographs, useful machine details, transparent status, displayed prices where approved, direct WhatsApp contact, physical viewing opportunities and simple navigation. Do not copy competitor content, keyword stuffing or unverified commercial claims. No competitor statistics or named-company rankings are asserted.

## Inventory and individual machine SEO

Make all published inventory reachable through crawlable links. Available machines lead, followed by Reserved and Sold; newest listings generally lead within Available. Support simple brand/model search and the specification's simple filters. Advanced filters remain future work.

Each physical machine needs its own stable identity, URL, actual photographs and factual information. Page identity should naturally combine year where available, brand, model and machinery type. Example only: 2018 Komatsu PC200-8 Used Excavator; this is not actual HGR inventory. Machine Location is useful where verified. Serial Number is optional, supports internal storage and is publicly displayed only when the owner/admin chooses. Distinguish similar units using verified year, hours, condition and identity where available. Do not fill unknown fields or generate repetitive descriptions to make pages longer.

The owner should get sensible metadata and URL defaults through administration. Routine publishing must not require manual SEO work or a developer deployment.

## Sold machines

Keep useful sold listings publicly accessible, clearly marked Sold, with retained factual details and links to available alternatives. Update snippets and any structured data so they do not advertise current availability. Sold history can serve customers and preserve useful URLs; continued ranking is not guaranteed. Do not automatically delete, redirect or noindex a page just because a unit sold. Preserve the existing unit URL; never reuse it for a different physical machine of the same model. Replace misleading availability actions with available-inventory navigation and similar-machine contact.

## Information architecture and internal links

Use the established navigation: Used Excavators, Rental, Machinery Services, Lubricants, About, Contact. The logo links home; a Home navigation item is unnecessary.

The homepage Available Excavators feed includes only Published + Available machines, approximately 3–6 or fewer if fewer are available. The full inventory retains all three statuses. Link homepage inventory to actual units, units back to inventory through breadcrumbs, and sold units to suitable available machines. Related-machine logic can remain simple. Rental links must state that availability requires enquiry. Use descriptive anchors rather than repeated exact-match keywords.

V1 does not include additional location, brand/model/category or informational/blog pages. Keep one primary rental page at /excavator-rental; do not create state/city rental pages. Keep Machinery Services broad until specific capabilities are confirmed, and Lubricants low priority until real products exist.

Future location, brand or category pages require sufficient inventory or real offerings, distinct intent, useful content and evidence from Search Console, keyword research, Ads or customer enquiries. [FUTURE] divisions require real offerings and their own appropriate content types.

## Content quality and duplication

Write concise introductions and keep inventory near the top. Place useful supporting information after machinery where appropriate. Public claims must be verified in Company Information and approved through Website Copy.

Avoid empty categories, copied manufacturer paragraphs, repeated city pages, fictional FAQs, invented reviews and keyword stuffing. Separate a repeatable product model from one physical used machine. Avoid competing pages for the same intent; maintain one page-to-keyword map.

## Organic and paid landing pages

Used-excavator Ads should normally land on the inventory page or a relevant actual unit. Rental and service campaigns need matching verified offerings. Share accurate inventory, fast mobile presentation and clear contact actions across paid and organic journeys.

Dedicated campaign pages are [FUTURE] unless justified separately. They must add a real purpose and avoid competing duplicate indexable pages. Tracking URLs should not create separate content identities.

## Mudah and owned machine pages

Confirmed: HGR will continue listing machinery on Mudah. Mudah remains an active customer-acquisition channel alongside the website, not a channel being replaced.

The HGR website is the primary owned source of complete machine information. Its individual pages should provide richer presentation through larger galleries, fuller specifications/descriptions, video where available, status, viewing details and direct contact.

Where Mudah's current policy permits, link each marketplace listing to the corresponding physical machine's HGR page. Do not hard-code a photo limit or presume external links/tracking parameters are always permitted.

Preferred journey: Mudah listing → matching HGR machine page → richer machine information/media → WhatsApp / call / enquiry → Arrange a Viewing if needed → offline negotiation/transaction.

Keep both inventories separately manageable. No technical dependency on Mudah, automatic publishing or synchronisation is required for V1. The owner/admin should easily obtain the public machine URL, for example through Copy Machine Link. Generating a tracked marketplace URL is an implementation consideration, not a selected solution.
