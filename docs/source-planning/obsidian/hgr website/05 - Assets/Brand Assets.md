# HGR Brand Assets

## Authority and scope

The approved direction comes from [[01 - Brand/Brand Guidelines]] and [[01 - Brand/Design System]]. This note records that system and asset requirements; it does not redesign the logo, invent colours or claim source files have been supplied.

## Logo masters and variants

Use the latest approved HGR logo with the excavator graphic and corrected arm position. Actual approved filename, version, source location and rights record: [TO CONFIRM].

Obtain a transparent master and the best available vector/editable source. Preserve source files unchanged. If only raster artwork exists, do not invent a vector master or trace/redesign the identity without approval.

Alternative light/dark, monochrome or compact variants may be used only if available and approved. Their existence and approved uses are [TO CONFIRM].

## Logo usage and clear space

Maintain original proportions, sharpness, contrast and sufficient surrounding space. Keep the logo distinguishable at header, footer and mobile sizes. The header logo links home.

The source guidelines do not specify a numeric clear-space formula or minimum size. Those exact values are [TO CONFIRM] through asset review; do not present an invented measurement as an approved rule.

Do not stretch, distort, recolour, add effects, alter the excavator arm, rebuild the lettering or place the logo over a background that makes it unreadable. Use an approved transparent version where appropriate.

## Typography

Inter is the established primary website typeface. Existing fallback: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif. Do not add decorative families merely for an industrial appearance.

Obtain the intended font files and verify their licence/version [TO CONFIRM]. Delivery, subsetting and loading details are [DEFERRED]. Use the Design System's responsive type scale and readable weights; do not modify the logo lettering to match the interface font.

## Icon direction

Use simple consistent icons that support familiar actions. Pair important contact/navigation icons with clear text, particularly WhatsApp, phone, menu and enquiry actions.

Specific icon family/source and licence are [DEFERRED]. Do not mix unrelated visual styles or redraw third-party brand marks inaccurately.

## Favicon and social sharing

Prepare favicon exports from approved identity, checking recognisability at small sizes. A simplified variant requires approval rather than automatic logo redesign. Exact export set follows implementation requirements [DEFERRED].

Prepare a default sharing asset using approved HGR identity and truthful imagery. Machine shares should use the actual featured image where appropriate. Test crop, contrast and legibility across intended sharing contexts; do not make an unsupported machine claim in a graphic.

## Web, print and source formats

- Preserve vector/editable originals when available, with approval/version records.
- Use suitable approved vector web exports for logo/icon artwork where supported.
- Use transparent raster exports where needed without pretending they are editable vector sources.
- Use appropriate compressed web derivatives for photography, separate from masters.
- Keep high-quality print/source files distinct from website derivatives; print colour/export requirements require confirmation with the intended production supplier.
- Do not overwrite a master with a compressed, cropped or watermarked derivative.

These are format directions, not a selection of production software or asset provider.

## Naming and preservation

Proposed naming pattern: hgr-[asset]-[approved-variant]-[version]-[purpose]. Record the actual source location, owner, approval date and permitted use alongside the asset inventory. Do not label an unapproved file final.

Use the approved logo source for configurable watermarks described in [[05 - Assets/Photography & Video]]. Keep machinery originals unwatermarked. The logo's presence on an image does not establish rights to that image.

## Future brand changes

An approved brand change should update central tokens and approved asset references, then trigger review of header/footer, favicon, sharing assets, watermarks, advertising and print materials. Do not silently replace the approved identity.
