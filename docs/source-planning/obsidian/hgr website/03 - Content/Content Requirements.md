# HGR Website Content Requirements

## 1. Purpose

This document is the master checklist of factual information, written content, photographs, videos, documents and business details required to complete the HGR Equipment Resources website.

This is not the final website copy.

Information should first be collected and verified before it is converted into public-facing website content.

Do not invent missing information.

---

## 2. Content Status System

When collecting content, information may be classified as:

- Confirmed
- To Confirm
- Not Applicable
- Future

Only confirmed information should be treated as approved factual website content.

---

## 3. Company Information

Confirm:

- Full business name
- Trading name
- Business registration information if required publicly
- Short company description
- Business history
- Year business started if intended for publication
- Owner name if intended for publication
- Owner role/title
- Relevant machinery experience
- Main business activities
- Primary geographic market
- Physical yard/location
- Areas served
- Business operating hours
- Appointment requirements if applicable

Do not invent company history, experience or statistics.

---

## 4. Contact Information

Confirm:

- Primary phone number
- WhatsApp number
- Business email
- Contact-form receiving email
- Full business address
- Google Business Profile
- Google Maps location
- Business operating hours
- Verified contact endpoints for the confirmed priority: WhatsApp, phone, then website enquiry/email

Contact information should be consistent throughout the website.

---

## 5. Used Excavator Content

For each machine, obtain where available:

- Brand
- Model
- Year
- Operating hours
- Engine
- Operating weight
- Condition
- Country of origin
- Price
- Availability status
- Description
- Machine location
- Serial Number (optional internal record, optional owner/admin-approved public display)
- Featured image
- Full photo gallery
- YouTube video URL if available
- Additional specifications where relevant

Machine status should support:

- Available
- Reserved
- Sold

Pricing should support:

- Public price
- Contact for Price

Do not require unavailable information simply to publish a machine.

Each physical unit needs a unique stable public URL; do not reuse sold-unit URLs. Use year where available, brand, model and machinery type naturally in page identity. Internal-only Serial Number must remain out of public copy, metadata, schema and links.

---

## 6. Machinery Photography

For each machine, aim to obtain useful real photographs such as:

- Front
- Rear
- Left side
- Right side
- Boom and arm
- Bucket
- Tracks / undercarriage
- Engine area
- Operator cabin
- Hour meter
- Identification / serial plate where appropriate
- Close-ups of relevant condition
- Additional useful machinery details

Photographs should accurately represent the machine.

Do not use AI-generated images as substitutes for actual machine listing photographs.

---

## 7. Machinery Video

Where available, obtain:

- Walkaround video
- Startup video
- Engine-running video
- Machinery operation video
- Other useful inspection footage

Public machinery video should normally be hosted through YouTube and connected to the machine listing through its video URL.

Video is optional and should not prevent a machine from being listed.

## 8. Rental Information

Confirmed model: Rental is available only when suitable machinery/opportunities exist; there may be no rental machines. Keep the public page enquiry-led with quotations and transactions offline. No permanent rental catalogue or online booking is required.

Confirm remaining details:

- Specific rental supply/operating arrangements for each enquiry
- Types of machines potentially available
- Geographic rental coverage
- Minimum rental period if applicable
- Whether operator is included or optional
- Transport arrangements
- Rental enquiry process
- Information required from customer
- Any rental conditions that should be communicated

Do not imply that every machine listed for sale is automatically available for rental.

Rental pricing is quotation/enquiry based for V1; do not publish fixed rental prices.

## 26. Content Priority

Content collection should follow this priority:

### Priority 1 — Required for V1

- Company information
- Contact information
- Used excavator inventory
- Machine photographs
- Machine pricing/status
- WhatsApp details
- Yard/location information
- Core homepage content
- Rental information
- Machinery Services information
- Lubricant enquiry-page information; actual products/stock are not a launch prerequisite
- Required legal content

### Priority 2 — Strongly Preferred

- Machine videos
- Yard photography
- Owner/business photography
- Customer reviews/testimonials
- Better company story
- Product documentation

### Priority 3 — Future

- Additional machinery categories
- Large lubricant catalogue
- New distributorship content
- Additional languages
- Advanced educational content

V1 should not be delayed by non-essential future content.

---

## 27. Content Completion Rule

A page should not be considered content-ready simply because placeholder text exists.

Content is ready when the important information is:

1. Collected
2. Verified
3. Written
4. Approved
5. Ready for publication

Placeholders should not reach the production website.
