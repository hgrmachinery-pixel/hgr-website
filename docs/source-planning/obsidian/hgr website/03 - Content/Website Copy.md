# HGR Website Copy

## 1. Purpose

This document contains the master customer-facing copy framework for the HGR Equipment Resources website.

It should eventually become the source of truth for public website wording.

Business facts must first be verified in:

`03 - Content/Company Information.md`

SEO targeting should be developed separately in:

`04 - SEO/`

The final copy should balance:

- clarity
- trust
- machinery expertise
- lead generation
- search relevance
- ease of understanding

---

# 2. Brand Voice

HGR should communicate in a way that is:

- Direct
- Practical
- Knowledgeable
- Approachable
- Professional
- Helpful
- Easy to understand

Avoid unnecessarily complicated machinery terminology where simpler wording will work.

Avoid generic corporate phrases such as:

- world-class solutions
- industry-leading excellence
- unparalleled service
- one-stop solution
- redefining the industry
- your trusted partner

unless a specific phrase is genuinely appropriate and supported.

HGR should sound like a knowledgeable machinery business speaking directly to a customer.

Prioritise WhatsApp, phone, then website enquiry/email. Machinery sales, rental and service quotations/transactions normally proceed offline; lubricant enquiries may progress offline in V1.

# 21. Meta Content Direction

Final SEO wording should follow the research-informed themes now recorded in:

`04 - SEO/Keyword Strategy.md`

External Malaysian qualitative findings and the human-approved strategy have been incorporated. Quantitative keyword volume, CPC, Ads competition, difficulty and forecasts remain uncollected; do not claim that Keyword Planner work is complete.

Use the approved commercial themes naturally and obtain final factual/copy approval. Prioritise /used-excavators, unique physical-machine pages and genuine Klang trust signals. No V1 blog or extra model/location pages are required.

Machine identity should use year where available, brand, model and machinery type without inventing missing data. Preserve sold-page identity and remove misleading availability copy; a new physical machine needs its own URL.

Mudah visitors may arrive directly on a unit page, so provide fuller genuine specifications, gallery, description, optional video, status, viewing and contact information without requiring a homepage visit. Do not hard-code marketplace policy claims.

English remains V1. Malay market terminology belongs in future Bahasa Malaysia research/localisation, not unnatural English-page keyword stuffing.

# 25. V1 Copy Principle

The V1 website should prioritise:

1. What HGR sells
2. Which machines are available
3. The important details of each machine
4. How much the machine costs where price is displayed
5. How to contact HGR
6. Where HGR is located
7. What other relevant services HGR actually provides

Customers should not have to read long corporate copy before reaching the machinery.
