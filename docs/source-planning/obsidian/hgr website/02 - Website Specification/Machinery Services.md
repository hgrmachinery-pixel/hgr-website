# Machinery Services Page Specification

## 1. Purpose

The Machinery Services page is a public enquiry route for machinery servicing/repair assistance that HGR may provide or arrange. Exact technical service scope remains [TO CONFIRM].

URL:

`/machinery-services`

The page should help customers:

1. Understand that they can contact HGR about heavy machinery service requirements
2. Explain their machine/problem without assuming a fixed service catalogue
3. Contact HGR regarding a machinery problem or servicing requirement
4. Provide enough information for HGR to understand the enquiry

The customer can use WhatsApp, phone or website enquiry/email, provide relevant details, discuss any necessary inspection, receive a quotation where appropriate and complete commercial arrangements offline. Do not require online service booking, fixed packages or checkout.

---

# 2. Naming

Navigation label:

**Machinery Services**

Primary page heading:

**Heavy Machinery Services**

The term "Machinery Services" is intentionally used instead of the broader "Services".

This allows the website to clearly communicate that the services relate specifically to machinery while allowing HGR to expand beyond excavators in the future.

---

# 3. Primary Customer Journey

The intended journey is:

Machinery Services

↓

Understand the Enquiry Process

↓

Describe Machine / Problem

↓

WhatsApp HGR / Call / Send Enquiry

↓

HGR Discusses Requirement / Inspection if Necessary

↓

Quotation Where Appropriate

↓

Offline Commercial Arrangements

Keep this process simple.

# 10. Service Enquiry Form

Provide a concise alternative to WhatsApp.

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Name | Required |
| Company | Optional |
| Phone | Required |
| Email | Optional |
| Machinery Type | Optional |
| Machine Brand / Model | Optional |
| Machine Location | Required |
| Description of Issue / Request (Message) | Required combined field; do not require a duplicate message |

CTA: **Send Enquiry**

The enquiry initiates discussion, not a service booking. Mirror these labels and optionality in Website Copy. Customers may send relevant photos through WhatsApp.

---

# 11. Photo Upload

The service enquiry form may support optional customer photo uploads.

This can allow customers to provide photographs of:

- Machine
- Damaged component
- Problem area
- Relevant machinery details

This feature should only be implemented if it can be handled securely and simply.

For V1, customers may alternatively send photographs directly through WhatsApp.

WhatsApp photo sharing may be sufficient initially.

---

# 12. Enquiry Delivery

For V1, service enquiries should be delivered to HGR by email.

Clearly identify the enquiry as:

**Machinery Service Enquiry**

Include:

- Customer name
- Company
- Phone
- Email
- Machinery type
- Machine brand / model
- Machine location
- Description of issue / request (message)

CRM integration is not required for V1.

---

# 13. Service Pricing

Do not publish standard servicing prices unless HGR later establishes fixed-price services.

Display:

**Enquire About Machinery Services**

Pricing may depend on:

- Machine
- Problem
- Required work
- Parts
- Machine location
- Other requirements

HGR should assess the enquiry before providing pricing.

---

# 14. Service Area

HGR should clearly communicate the actual geographic coverage of its machinery services.

Do not automatically assume that machinery servicing is available throughout all of Malaysia simply because machinery sales enquiries are accepted nationwide.

Service coverage should be confirmed before final website copy is published.

---

# 15. Emergency / Urgent Service

Do not advertise:

- 24-hour service
- Emergency repair
- Same-day repair
- Guaranteed response time

unless HGR can consistently provide it.

Customers with urgent requirements may be encouraged to call or WhatsApp HGR.

---

# 16. Mobile Experience

Mobile should prioritise:

1. Understand service
2. WhatsApp HGR
3. Call HGR
4. Submit service enquiry

Buttons should be large and easy to use.

Avoid long technical explanations.

---

# 17. SEO

Research-informed themes include excavator repair Malaysia, excavator service Malaysia and heavy machinery services Malaysia. They are research themes only until HGR verifies the specific capabilities; no quantitative metrics are asserted.

Retain the broad /machinery-services page and Heavy Machinery Services heading. Do not create hydraulic repair, engine overhaul, undercarriage repair or other specific service pages before owner confirmation and useful content justify them.

The journey remains enquiry → discussion → inspection if necessary → quotation → offline transaction. Future SEO expansion must follow actual capabilities and evidence.

# 19. Google Ads

If HGR later advertises machinery servicing through Google Ads, relevant advertisements should direct visitors to this page or an appropriate dedicated service page.

Sales advertisements should continue directing customers toward machinery inventory.

Rental advertisements should direct customers toward the Rental page.

This keeps advertising intent separated.

# 21. Design Principle

The Machinery Services page should not attempt to make HGR appear like a large service centre if that does not reflect the actual business.

The page should communicate:

- What support HGR provides
- What machine the customer has
- What problem they need help with
- How to contact HGR

Keep the presentation professional, practical and direct.

---

# 22. Success Criteria

The Machinery Services page is successful when a customer can quickly answer:

**Can I contact HGR about machinery servicing/repair assistance?**

**Can HGR potentially help with my machine?**

**What information should I provide?**

**How do I contact HGR?**

The customer should be able to make a useful service enquiry within approximately one minute.
