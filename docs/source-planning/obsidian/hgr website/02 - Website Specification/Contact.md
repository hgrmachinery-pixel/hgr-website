# Contact Page Specification

## 1. Purpose

The Contact page provides customers with the simplest possible way to contact HGR Equipment Resources.

URL:

`/contact`

The page should support:

- WhatsApp
- Phone
- Contact form
- Physical location
- Google Maps / directions

The page should not contain unnecessary marketing content.

---

# 2. Primary Customer Journey

The intended journey is:

Contact Page

↓

Choose Contact Method

↓

WhatsApp / Call / Send Enquiry / Get Directions

The customer should be able to initiate contact within seconds.

---

# 3. Page Structure

Recommended V1 structure:

1. Header
2. Contact Introduction
3. Primary Contact Methods
4. General Enquiry Form
5. HGR Location / Yard
6. Google Map
7. Footer

Keep the page concise.

---

# 4. Page Heading

Suggested heading:

**Contact HGR Equipment Resources**

Supporting message direction:

**Looking for machinery or need more information? Contact HGR directly.**

Final wording will be handled during the Content phase.

---

# 5. Primary Contact Methods

The most important contact methods should appear near the top of the page.

Recommended priority:

### WhatsApp

**WhatsApp HGR**

### Phone

**Call HGR**

### Enquiry

**Send Enquiry**

### Location

**Get Directions**

Use clear labels rather than icons alone.

---

# 6. WhatsApp

WhatsApp should be one of the most prominent contact methods.

For a general Contact-page enquiry, suggested message:

**Hi HGR, I'd like to enquire about your machinery/products.**

The customer can then continue the conversation.

Machine-specific pages should continue using machine-specific WhatsApp messages.

Rental and Machinery Services pages should use their own enquiry messages.

---

# 7. Phone

Display HGR's verified business phone number.

On supported devices, the number should be clickable.

CTA:

**Call HGR**

The phone number should also be displayed as readable text where appropriate.

---

# 8. Email

Display HGR's verified business email address if HGR wants customers to contact the business directly by email.

The email address should be clickable where appropriate.

Email should generally remain secondary to WhatsApp and phone for customer enquiries.

---

# 9. General Enquiry Form

Recommended fields:

### Name

Required

### Company

Optional

### Phone

Required

### Email

Optional

### Machine / Subject of Enquiry

Optional

### Message

Required

CTA:

**Send Enquiry**

Keep the form short.

---

# 10. Enquiry Type

The form may include a simple optional selector:

**I'm interested in:**

- Used Excavator
- Excavator Rental
- Machinery Services
- Lubricants
- General Enquiry

This may help HGR understand the enquiry immediately.

Do not create a complicated multi-step form.

---

# 11. Enquiry Delivery

For V1, general enquiries should be delivered to HGR by email.

The email should clearly contain:

- Enquiry type
- Customer name
- Company
- Phone
- Email
- Machine / subject of enquiry
- Message
- Source page where practical

The email subject should make the enquiry easy to identify.

Example:

**HGR Website Enquiry - Used Excavator**

---

# 12. Form Confirmation

After successful submission, clearly confirm that the enquiry was received.

Example direction:

**Thank you. Your enquiry has been sent to HGR.**

Then provide another direct option:

**Need a faster response? WhatsApp HGR.**

Do not leave the customer wondering whether the form worked.

---

# 13. Form Errors

Errors should use simple language.

Example:

**Please enter your phone number.**

Avoid technical error messages.

If submission fails, provide:

**WhatsApp HGR**

and

**Call HGR**

so the customer still has a way to contact the business.

---

# 14. Spam Protection

The contact form should include appropriate anti-spam protection.

Protection should minimise friction for genuine customers.

Avoid difficult CAPTCHAs unless genuinely necessary.

The technical anti-spam method will be selected during development.

---

# 15. HGR Location

Include HGR's physical business/yard location.

Suggested heading:

**Visit HGR in Klang**

Display verified information such as:

- Business name
- Address
- Klang, Selangor
- Phone
- Business hours if confirmed

Do not publish unverified information.

---

# 16. Google Map

Include a Google Map or appropriate map experience showing HGR's verified location.

Provide:

**Get Directions**

The map should not unnecessarily slow down the initial page load.

A lightweight map preview or delayed map loading may be considered during development.

---

# 17. Machinery Inspection

Customers can view/inspect machinery at the Klang yard, preferably by appointment.

Supporting direction:

**Contact HGR to arrange a machine viewing at our Klang yard. Confirm the machine's location before visiting.**

CTA:

**Arrange a Viewing**

WhatsApp is a prominent route, with phone and enquiry/email alternatives. No mandatory online appointment form or scheduling system is needed. Viewing and subsequent negotiation/purchase take place offline.

Do not imply that every listed machine is at the yard. Full address and business/visiting hours remain [TO CONFIRM].

---

# 18. Business Hours

Business hours may be displayed if HGR has confirmed operating hours.

Do not invent business hours.

If operating hours vary or are appointment-based, communicate this accurately.

---

# 19. Mobile Experience

The Contact page should be especially simple on mobile.

Priority:

1. WhatsApp
2. Call
3. Enquiry Form
4. Directions

Contact buttons should be large and easy to tap.

The phone number and WhatsApp action should not be hidden inside complicated navigation.

---

# 20. Persistent Contact

The broader website may use a persistent WhatsApp action.

If present on the Contact page, ensure it does not unnecessarily duplicate or obstruct the primary contact controls.

---

# 21. Contact Source Tracking

Where practical, HGR should be able to understand where an enquiry originated.

Potential sources:

- Homepage
- Used Excavators
- Individual Machine
- Rental
- Machinery Services
- Lubricants
- About
- Contact
- Google Ads

This may be handled through analytics and/or enquiry metadata.

Include **Mudah** and distinguish Google Organic, Google Ads, Direct and other referrals where practical. Preserve permitted campaign/referral context through machine views, WhatsApp/phone actions, form submissions and Arrange a Viewing. Exact tracking/UTM implementation remains [DEFERRED].

Website enquiries and clicks are not proof of completed offline sales or their acquisition source. Any sale attribution requires an appropriate future manual/offline conversion process.

---

# 22. Google Ads Tracking

Relevant contact actions should support conversion tracking.

Potential conversions:

- WhatsApp click
- Phone click
- General enquiry submitted
- Machine enquiry submitted
- Rental enquiry submitted
- Machinery service enquiry submitted
- Lubricant enquiry submitted

The exact conversion setup will be handled during analytics and advertising implementation.

---

# 23. SEO

The Contact page should contain consistent business information.

Important information includes:

- HGR Equipment Resources
- Address
- Phone
- Location
- Contact information

Business details should remain consistent with HGR's verified online business information.

---

# 24. Privacy

The enquiry form collects customer information.

The website should explain how submitted information is used where legally/operationally appropriate.

A Privacy Policy should be available.

Do not request unnecessary personal information.

---

# 25. Accessibility

The primary conversion priority is WhatsApp, phone, then website enquiry/email. Machinery sales, rental and service quotations/transactions normally proceed offline; lubricant enquiries may proceed offline in V1.

Contact methods and forms should support:

- Clear labels
- Keyboard navigation
- Large touch targets
- Strong contrast
- Useful validation messages
- Visible focus states
- Accessible form fields

Do not rely on placeholder text as the only field label.

---

# 26. Performance

The Contact page should remain lightweight.

Particular attention should be given to embedded maps.

Do not allow a heavy map implementation to significantly delay access to:

- WhatsApp
- Phone
- Contact form

Primary contact information should load before non-essential map functionality.

---

# 27. Design Principle

The Contact page should not attempt to impress the visitor.

Its job is to make communication easy.

The visitor should immediately understand:

**WhatsApp HGR**

**Call HGR**

**Send Enquiry**

**Visit HGR**

Everything else is secondary.

---

# 28. Content Required Before Launch

Confirm before launch:

- Business name
- Phone number
- WhatsApp number
- Email address
- Full address
- Google Business location
- Business hours if displayed
- Contact-form receiving email

These details must be verified before publication.

---

# 29. Success Criteria

The Contact page is successful when a visitor can:

**WhatsApp HGR**

within seconds,

or:

**Call HGR**

within seconds,

or:

**Submit a general enquiry**

without unnecessary fields,

or:

**Find HGR's location**

without difficulty.

The Contact page should remove friction rather than introduce it.
