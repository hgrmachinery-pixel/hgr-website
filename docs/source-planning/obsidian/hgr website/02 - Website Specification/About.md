# About HGR Page Specification

## 1. Purpose

The About page introduces HGR Equipment Resources and builds confidence in the business.

URL:

`/about`

The page should help visitors understand:

1. Who HGR is
2. What HGR does
3. Where HGR operates
4. How HGR works with customers
5. Where HGR is physically located
6. How to contact HGR

The page should feel genuine and practical rather than overly corporate.

---

# 2. Primary Message

HGR Equipment Resources is a Malaysia-based machinery business specialising primarily in used excavators.

Secondary opportunities include availability-based excavator rental, enquiry-led Machinery Services assistance and request/supply-based lubricants. These remain secondary to used excavator sales. Future machinery divisions must not be advertised before they exist.

The exact public wording will be developed during the Content phase.

---

# 3. Brand Positioning

The About page should present HGR as:

- Professional
- Knowledgeable
- Practical
- Approachable
- Straightforward
- Personal
- Machinery-focused

HGR should not attempt to present itself as a large corporation if that does not reflect the actual business.

The personal nature of the business can be a strength.

---

# 4. Page Structure

Recommended V1 structure:

1. Header
2. About HGR Hero
3. HGR Story / Introduction
4. What HGR Does
5. How HGR Works
6. HGR Yard / Klang
7. Future / Broader Machinery Capability where appropriate
8. Contact CTA
9. Footer

Keep the page concise.

---

# 5. Hero

Suggested heading:

**About HGR Equipment Resources**

Supporting message direction:

**Practical machinery solutions with direct, personal service.**

The hero should preferably use:

- Real HGR machinery
- HGR yard photography
- Owner/business photography where appropriate

Avoid generic stock imagery where real HGR photography is available.

---

# 6. HGR Introduction

Provide a short explanation of the business.

The introduction should explain:

- HGR Equipment Resources
- Used machinery focus
- Used excavator specialisation
- Malaysia market
- Klang, Selangor base
- Direct customer communication

Avoid unnecessarily long company history.

---

# 7. HGR Story

The website may include a short company story.

Potential subjects:

- How HGR started
- Experience in machinery
- Why HGR focuses on used machinery
- How HGR works with customers

The story should be based on factual information provided by the HGR owner.

Do not invent:

- Founding stories
- Years of experience
- Number of machines sold
- Customer numbers
- Industry achievements

These details should only be published when verified.

---

# 8. What HGR Does

Provide a clear overview of HGR's business areas.

### Used Excavators

HGR's primary website focus.

CTA:

**View Excavators**

### Excavator Rental

Rental may be available when suitable machinery or opportunities exist. There may be no rental stock; customers enquire and arrangements proceed offline.

CTA:

**Enquire About Rental**

### Machinery Services

HGR may provide or arrange assistance on an enquiry, discussion/inspection where necessary and quotation basis. Exact technical scope remains [TO CONFIRM]; do not publish specific unverified services.

CTA:

**Enquire About Machinery Services**

### Lubricants

Lubricants may be available upon request or supply opportunity. Brands/products remain [TO CONFIRM]; no permanent stock or V1 catalogue is implied.

CTA:

**Enquire About Lubricants**

These areas may evolve as the business expands.

---

# 9. Future Product Divisions

HGR may expand into additional machinery categories or become a dealer/distributor for machinery brands.

The About page should be capable of evolving as the business expands.

Do not advertise future distributorships or product categories until they actually exist.

The website architecture should allow HGR's business description to evolve without requiring a redesign.

---

# 10. Personal Service

One of HGR's differentiating characteristics is direct communication.

The website may communicate that customers can speak directly with HGR regarding:

- Machinery
- Availability
- Inspections
- Rental
- Machinery services
- Lubricants

Avoid creating the impression of a large call centre or corporate sales organisation.

---

# 11. Trust Through Evidence

The About page should build trust using real evidence.

Examples:

- Real HGR yard
- Real machinery
- Real machinery photographs
- Physical business location
- Google Business presence
- Direct phone contact
- Direct WhatsApp contact
- Actual business information

Prefer evidence over unsupported marketing claims.

---

# 12. Claims

Avoid unsupported statements such as:

- Malaysia's No.1 machinery dealer
- Best excavator dealer
- Largest machinery supplier
- Cheapest machinery
- Most trusted machinery company
- Highest quality machinery

Unless a claim can be objectively substantiated, do not use it.

Use factual information instead.

---

# 13. HGR Yard

Include a section introducing HGR's physical location.

Suggested heading:

**Visit HGR in Klang**

Potential content:

- Klang, Selangor
- Yard photography
- Google Map
- Directions
- Contact details
- Inspection information

The exact address should use verified HGR business information.

---

# 14. Machinery Inspection

Customers can arrange machinery viewing/inspection at the Klang yard. Appointments are preferred.

CTA:

**Arrange a Viewing**

Use WhatsApp prominently to arrange a viewing, with phone or enquiry as alternatives. Confirm the machine's location before visiting; not every listed machine is at the yard.

No online booking form or appointment scheduling system is required. Negotiation/purchase continues offline after viewing.

---

# 15. Owner / Team

Because HGR is owner-operated, the website may include the owner where appropriate.

Potential presentation:

- Owner photograph
- Name
- Role
- Short introduction

This is optional.

If used, the presentation should feel professional and personal rather than like a large corporate management-team page.

---

# 16. Photography

Prioritise authentic HGR photography.

Useful photographs may include:

- HGR yard
- Excavators
- Machinery inspection
- Owner with machinery
- Machinery loading / preparation
- Workshop/service activity where relevant
- Lubricant products where relevant

Photography should help demonstrate that HGR is a real operating machinery business.

---

# 17. Google Business / Map

Where appropriate, integrate or link to HGR's verified Google Business location.

Potential actions:

**Get Directions**

**View Location**

The website should use verified location information before launch.

---

# 18. Contact

The About page should provide easy contact access.

Primary:

**WhatsApp HGR**

Secondary:

**Call HGR**

Additional:

**Contact Us**

The visitor should not need to navigate away simply to find contact information.

---

# 19. Mobile Experience

Mobile should remain concise.

Priority:

1. Understand HGR
2. Understand what HGR does
3. See real business/machinery imagery
4. Locate HGR
5. Contact HGR

Avoid large walls of company-profile text.

---

# 20. SEO

The About page should naturally reinforce:

- HGR Equipment Resources
- Machinery business
- Used excavators
- Klang
- Selangor
- Malaysia

Do not turn the About page into an SEO keyword page.

Its primary role is business identity and trust.

---

# 21. Structured Business Information

Where technically appropriate, the website should provide structured business information that can help search engines understand HGR.

This may include relevant organisation/local business information.

Exact structured-data implementation will be determined during SEO and technical development.

---

# 22. Analytics

Potential events:

- View Excavators clicked
- Rental clicked
- Machinery Services clicked
- Lubricants clicked
- WhatsApp clicked
- Phone clicked
- Directions clicked
- Arrange a Viewing clicked

Analytics should focus on meaningful customer actions.

---

# 23. Design Principle

The About page should communicate:

**HGR is a real machinery business run by real people.**

Trust should come primarily from:

**Real Machinery + Real Location + Clear Information + Direct Communication**

rather than excessive corporate language.

---

# 24. Content Requirements Before Launch

Before final About-page copy is written, confirm:

- Correct registered/trading business name
- Business history
- Owner information if displayed
- Relevant machinery experience
- Exact yard address
- Contact information
- Actual service capabilities
- Actual geographic coverage
- Any factual company statistics intended for publication

Do not invent missing information.

---

# 25. Success Criteria

The About page is successful when a visitor can quickly answer:

**Who is HGR?**

**What does HGR do?**

**Is this a real machinery business?**

**Where is HGR located?**

**Can I visit or inspect machinery?**

**Who am I dealing with?**

**How do I contact HGR?**

The page should increase confidence without requiring the visitor to read a long corporate profile.
