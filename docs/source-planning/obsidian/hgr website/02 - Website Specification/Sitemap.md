# HGR Website Sitemap

## 1. Sitemap Objective

The website structure should make it extremely easy for visitors to:

1. Understand what HGR sells
2. View available machinery
3. Find a suitable machine
4. View machine information
5. Contact HGR

The primary website journey should require as few steps as possible.

The website should also provide a scalable structure for future SEO, Google Ads and additional machinery categories.

---

# 2. Primary Navigation

The main desktop navigation should initially contain:

**Used Excavators**

**Rental**

**Machinery Services**

**Lubricants**

**About**

**Contact**

The HGR logo should link to the homepage.

A prominent **View Excavators** or equivalent inventory action may also be used where appropriate.

On mobile, the same navigation should be presented in a simple mobile menu.

---

## Used Excavators

URL:

`/used-excavators`

Purpose:

Main machinery inventory page.

Primary actions:

**View Machine**

**WhatsApp HGR**

This page should also be suitable as a primary destination for relevant Google Ads traffic.

---

## Individual Excavator

Proposed URL structure:

`/used-excavators/[machine-slug]`

Example:

`/used-excavators/komatsu-pc200-8-2018`

The exact URL structure should be confirmed during SEO and technical planning.

Purpose:

Provide detailed information about an individual machine.

Content may include:

- Brand
- Model
- Year
- Hours
- Engine
- Operating weight
- Condition
- Country of origin
- Price
- Status
- Description
- Photo gallery
- YouTube video
- Related machinery

Primary action:

**WhatsApp About This Machine**

Secondary actions:

**Call HGR**

**Send Enquiry**

WhatsApp enquiries should automatically reference the machine and include the machine page URL.

Each physical unit receives its own stable URL. Never reuse a sold-machine URL for another physical machine of the same model. Year where known, brand, model and machinery type should inform natural page identity; slug-generation details remain [DEFERRED].

Optional Serial Number supports internal storage and owner-controlled public display. It is not a required public fact or a substitute for stable internal Machine ID.

Where marketplace policy permits, a Mudah listing links directly to this corresponding unit page. No automatic marketplace publishing/synchronisation or website dependency on Mudah is required.

# 4. Excavator Rental

URL:

`/excavator-rental`

Keep this public V1 page enquiry-led. HGR intends to offer rental when suitable machinery or opportunities are available, and there may be no rental machines at a given time.

Customers provide their requirements and contact HGR to check current availability and obtain a quotation. Rental negotiations/transactions proceed offline. No permanent rental catalogue, online booking or public rental price is required.

If sales machinery is displayed, distinguish it from confirmed rental availability; no sales unit is automatically rentable.

Primary CTA: **Enquire About Rental**

Supporting actions: **WhatsApp About Rental**, **Call HGR**, **Send Enquiry**.

---

# 5. Machinery Services

URL:

`/machinery-services`

Public name: **Machinery Services**. Heading: **Heavy Machinery Services**.

HGR may provide or arrange service/repair assistance. Customers contact HGR, describe the machine/problem, provide details and discuss inspection and quotation where appropriate. Commercial arrangements proceed offline.

Exact technical scope remains [TO CONFIRM]. Do not present unverified service lists as a fixed catalogue or require online service booking/checkout.

Primary CTA: **Enquire About Machinery Services**

Supporting actions: **WhatsApp HGR**, **Call HGR**, **Send Enquiry**.

Individual verified service pages remain future scope if justified.

# 6. Lubricants

`/lubricants`

Customers can enquire via WhatsApp, phone or form and complete transactions offline.

The website architecture should allow the owner to add catalogue/enquiry products when available or in a future phase. [FUTURE] Selected-product online purchasing may be added without a full website rebuild; no checkout/payment/shipping technology is selected or required now.

**Enquire About Lubricants**

# 11. Future SEO Landing Pages

SEO research may identify valuable search categories.

Potential examples could include:

`/used-excavator-malaysia`

`/used-komatsu-excavators`

`/used-hitachi-excavators`

`/used-caterpillar-excavators`

However, these pages should only be created where there is a genuine search purpose and sufficient useful content.

SEO pages should not simply duplicate the main machinery listing page.

Final SEO landing pages will be decided during the SEO phase.

The approved V1 strategy is inventory-led: no extra brand/model/category or location landing pages and no blog are required. Do not automatically create the example routes above.

Future model pages, such as /used-excavators/komatsu-pc200, need sufficient inventory, unique useful content and evidence from Search Console, keyword research, Ads or enquiries. Keep one rental page in V1, not multiple state/city variants.

---

# 12. Google Ads Landing Pages

Google Ads should send visitors to the page most closely matching their search intent.

Example:

Search:

**used excavator for sale malaysia**

Destination:

**Used Excavators**

Search:

**excavator rental malaysia**

Destination:

**Excavator Rental**

Search:

**excavator repair / machinery servicing**

Destination:

**Machinery Services**

Dedicated advertising landing pages may be created later when there is a clear conversion or campaign reason.

Google Ads traffic should not automatically be sent to the homepage.

---

# 13. Homepage Relationship

The homepage should act as a simple gateway.

The primary route should be:

Home

↓

Used Excavators

↓

Individual Machine

↓

WhatsApp / Call / Enquiry

The homepage should not create unnecessary steps between the visitor and inventory. Its Available Excavators section shows only Published + Available machines, approximately 3–6 or fewer if fewer are available. Reserved/Sold remain on the full inventory page, not in that homepage section.

The final homepage conversion section prioritises WhatsApp HGR, then Call HGR, with Send Enquiry optional. Earlier homepage discovery sections retain prominent View Excavators actions.

---

# 14. Inventory Relationship

The Used Excavators page is the main product discovery page.

Expected hierarchy:

Used Excavators

├── Available Machine

├── Available Machine

├── Reserved Machine

└── Sold Machine

Available machinery should normally receive the highest visibility.

Reserved machinery may remain visible with a clear status.

Sold machinery should remain publicly accessible but should appear below active inventory where appropriate.

Keeping sold machine pages may also provide useful historical and SEO value.

---

- Video URL

The exact admin URL and technical implementation will be decided during technical architecture.
