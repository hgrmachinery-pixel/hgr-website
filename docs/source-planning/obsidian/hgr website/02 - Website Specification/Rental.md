# Excavator Rental Page Specification

## 1. Purpose

The Excavator Rental page remains a public V1 enquiry page. HGR intends to offer rental when suitable machinery or opportunities are available, rather than maintaining guaranteed permanent rental inventory. There may be periods with no excavators available for rent.

URL:

`/excavator-rental`

The page should:

1. Explain that HGR provides excavator rental enquiries
2. Help customers understand what information HGR needs
3. Allow customers to contact HGR quickly
4. Generate qualified rental enquiries
5. Optionally show relevant machinery without implying guaranteed rental availability

Rental pricing will not be published initially.

Customers should contact HGR for availability and quotation. Rental negotiations and transactions take place offline. V1 requires neither a permanent rental catalogue nor online booking.

---

# 2. Primary Customer Journey

The intended journey is:

Rental Page

↓

Understand Rental Service

↓

Provide Basic Project Requirements

↓

WhatsApp HGR / Send Enquiry

↓

HGR Confirms Suitable Machine, Availability and Price

The process should remain simple.

---

# 3. Page Structure

Recommended V1 structure:

1. Header
2. Rental Hero
3. Rental Requirements / How It Works
4. Potential Machinery
5. Rental Enquiry
6. Klang / Service Area Information
7. Final WhatsApp CTA
8. Footer

Avoid creating a complicated online rental booking system.

---

# 4. Hero

Suggested heading:

**Excavator Rental**

Supporting message direction:

**Excavator rental may be available depending on current machinery availability. Contact HGR with your project requirements to check current availability.**

Primary CTA:

**WhatsApp About Rental**

Secondary CTA:

**Send Enquiry**

Final wording will be handled during the Content phase.

---

# 5. Rental Availability

Rental availability is enquiry-based and may be zero. The page must remain useful as an enquiry route without a stock list. Never imply guaranteed or permanent rental stock.

Do not imply that every excavator displayed on the website is available for rental.

Avoid statements such as:

**All machines available for rental**

unless this is factually true.

Instead use wording such as:

**Contact HGR to check current rental availability.**

---

# 6. Rental Pricing

Rental prices should not be publicly displayed for V1.

Use the enquiry action:

**Enquire About Rental**

Explain that pricing is by quotation after HGR reviews availability and project requirements.

Pricing may depend on factors such as:

- Machine required
- Project requirements
- Rental duration
- Project location
- Transportation
- Other operational requirements

The exact quotation process is handled directly by HGR.

---

# 7. Rental Requirements

Help customers provide useful information before contacting HGR.

Suggested information:

### Machine Required

Brand/model if known, or general excavator requirement.

### Project Location

Where the excavator will be used.

### Rental Duration

Approximate rental period.

### Required Date

When the machine is needed.

### Project / Work Type

Short description of the work.

These fields should not make the enquiry process feel complicated.

---

# 8. How It Works

Provide a simple three-step explanation.

### 1. Tell Us What You Need

Provide the machine requirement, location and rental period.

### 2. HGR Checks Availability

HGR confirms whether a suitable machine is available.

### 3. Discuss Arrangements Offline

If suitable machinery is available, HGR discusses details and provides a quotation where appropriate. Negotiations and any rental transaction are completed offline; enquiry does not guarantee a rental.

Keep this section visually simple.

---

# 9. Potential Machinery

The rental page may display selected sales machinery as potential enquiry context, but this is optional and must not become a required permanent rental catalogue. Label sales availability separately from rental availability. A sales status of Available does not mean Available for Rental.

Suggested heading:

**Machines You May Be Interested In**

However, machinery displayed here must not automatically be labelled:

**Available for Rental**

unless rental availability has specifically been confirmed.

Use wording such as:

**Enquire About Rental**

This prevents customers from assuming that every sales machine can be rented.

---

# 10. Future Rental Availability Field

The machinery administration system may later support a dedicated rental setting.

Possible options:

- Not Available for Rental
- Rental Enquiry Accepted
- Available for Rental

This is not required for V1 unless HGR decides to actively maintain rental availability through the admin system.

For V1, rental availability may remain enquiry-based.

---

# 13. Enquiry Delivery

For V1, rental enquiries should be delivered to HGR by email.

The enquiry should clearly identify:

**Rental Enquiry**

Include:

- Customer name
- Company
- Phone
- Email
- Excavator requirement
- Project location
- Rental period
- Expected start date
- Type of work / message

CRM integration is not required for V1.

---

# 14. Service Area

HGR's machinery sales target market is Malaysia and its physical base is Klang, Selangor. Rental enquiries can include the customer's project location, but actual rental coverage and arrangements remain [TO CONFIRM].

Do not make unsupported claims about delivery, transport pricing or machine availability for every location.

Customers should contact HGR for project-specific arrangements.

---

# 15. Transportation

Transportation requirements may affect rental arrangements.

The website does not need to calculate machinery transportation charges.

Where appropriate, state:

**Contact HGR to discuss machine transportation and project location.**

Transport arrangements and pricing should be confirmed directly with HGR.

---

# 16. Phone Contact

Provide:

**Call HGR**

Some customers may prefer calling rather than completing a form.

Phone contact should remain easy to access, especially on mobile.

---

# 17. Mobile Experience

The rental page should be highly usable on mobile.

Priority:

1. Understand rental service
2. WhatsApp HGR
3. Provide project requirements
4. Send enquiry

Avoid long blocks of text.

Use large buttons and readable typography.

---

# 18. SEO

Research-informed themes: excavator rental Malaysia and excavator rental Selangor. These are not volume-ranked and do not establish HGR's actual coverage or guaranteed stock.

Retain one primary /excavator-rental page for V1. Do not create state/city rental pages, imply permanent inventory or treat all sales machines as rentable. Keep enquiry/quotation wording and no public fixed pricing for V1.

Future rental SEO expansion requires genuine capability/inventory and supporting data. Exact availability, coverage and terms remain [TO CONFIRM] per enquiry.

---

# 19. Google Ads

The Rental page may later serve as a landing page for rental-related advertising.

Rental search traffic should be directed here rather than automatically sending visitors to the homepage.

Advertising campaigns should only make claims that match HGR's actual rental capabilities.

---

# 20. Analytics

Potential events:

- WhatsApp Rental clicked
- Call clicked
- Rental enquiry started
- Rental enquiry submitted
- Machine viewed from Rental page

These events should allow rental leads to be distinguished from machinery sales enquiries.

---

# 21. No Online Booking for V1

Do not build:

- Rental calendar
- Online availability calendar
- Instant rental booking
- Online rental payment
- Automatic rental quotation calculator
- Customer rental accounts
- Permanent rental catalogue

unless HGR later identifies a genuine need.

V1 should generate enquiries rather than automate the complete rental transaction.

---

# 22. Design Principle

The Rental page should answer:

**Do you provide excavator rental enquiries?**

Yes.

**Can I see what may be available?**

Relevant machinery may be shown, but availability must be confirmed.

**How much does it cost?**

Contact HGR for a quotation.

**What information should I provide?**

Machine requirement, project location, rental duration and required date.

**How do I contact HGR?**

WhatsApp, phone or rental enquiry form.

The page should make these answers obvious while explaining that suitable machinery may not currently be available.

---

# 23. Success Criteria

The Rental page is successful when a customer can submit a useful rental enquiry within approximately one minute.

The customer should not need to understand the website or navigate through multiple pages before contacting HGR.
