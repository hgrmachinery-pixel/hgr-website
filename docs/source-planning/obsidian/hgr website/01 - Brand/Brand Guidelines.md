# HGR Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand

**Brand Name:** HGR Equipment Resources

**Website:** hgrmachinery.com

**Primary Business:** Used excavator sales

**Market:** Malaysia

HGR Equipment Resources should be presented as a professional, practical and approachable machinery business.

The brand should not attempt to appear like a large corporate machinery group.

HGR is owner-operated, and the website should use this as a strength by creating a more direct and personal buying experience.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

HGR should feel:

- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Practical
- Experienced
- Straightforward
- Approachable
- Machinery-focused
- Modern without appearing overly technological

The website should communicate:

> Real machines. Clear information. Direct communication.

This is a brand direction rather than a public slogan and does not need to appear throughout the website.

---

## 3. Target Customer

Primary customers include:

- Construction companies
- Contractors
- Machinery operators
- Factories
- Business owners
- Individual machinery buyers

Some visitors may not be highly tech-savvy.

The brand experience must therefore prioritise:

**Clarity > Creativity**

**Usability > Complexity**

**Real information > Marketing language**

**Machines > Corporate presentation**

---

## 4. Logo

Use the latest approved HGR logo featuring the excavator graphic with the corrected excavator arm position.

The logo should be used consistently across:

- Website
- Social media
- Documents
- Advertising
- Signage
- Future marketing materials

### Logo Usage

The logo must:

- Remain clearly visible
- Maintain its original proportions
- Have sufficient surrounding space
- Maintain strong contrast against its background

Do not:

- Stretch the logo
- Distort the logo
- Add unnecessary effects
- Place it over visually complicated backgrounds where it becomes difficult to read

Transparent versions should be used where appropriate.

---

## 5. Colour Direction

The website should initially retain the existing HGR colour direction established during the previous HGR design work.

The colour system must be implemented using reusable design variables rather than colours being manually hard-coded throughout the website.

This is important because HGR may change or refine its colour palette in the future.

A future colour change should therefore be achievable primarily by changing the central design system rather than redesigning every page.

Exact colour values will be documented separately in:

**Design System**

---

## 6. Light and Dark Design

The website should use a combination of light and dark sections.

The majority of machinery browsing and information areas should favour clean, light backgrounds because they improve:

- Readability
- Machinery visibility
- Specification readability
- Product comparison
- General usability

Dark sections may be used selectively to create:

- Premium visual contrast
- Industrial character
- Strong section transitions
- Hero moments
- Important calls to action

The website should not become predominantly dark.

The objective is:

> Clean modern machinery marketplace + premium industrial presentation.

---

## 7. Typography

The primary website typeface should be:

**Inter**

Inter is selected because it provides strong screen readability and works well for:

- Machinery model numbers
- Prices
- Specifications
- Buttons
- Navigation
- Headings
- Body content

Typography should be clean and highly legible.

Avoid decorative or overly industrial fonts for normal website content.

Industrial character should primarily come from machinery photography, layout and visual presentation rather than difficult-to-read typography.

### Typography Principles

Use:

- Large headings
- Clear section titles
- Comfortable body text
- Large readable buttons
- Strong price presentation
- Clear machinery specifications
- Strong contrast

Avoid:

- Very small text
- Thin low-contrast text
- Long dense paragraphs
- Excessive uppercase text
- Decorative typography

Exact typography sizes will be defined in the Design System.

---

## 8. Photography

Real machinery photography should be the primary visual content of the HGR website.

Machine listing pages must use photographs of the actual machine being sold.

Photography may be professionally enhanced or corrected where appropriate.

Acceptable editing includes:

- Exposure correction
- Colour correction
- Contrast correction
- Straightening
- Cropping
- Background cleanup
- Minor distraction removal
- Image sharpening
- Image optimisation

Editing must not materially misrepresent the machine.

Important physical details, machine condition, components or damage should not be artificially changed in a way that could mislead a buyer.

---

## 9. AI and Creative Image Editing

AI tools may be used to improve marketing presentation and create promotional assets.

Tools such as Higgsfield may be used during the creative production process.

However:

**AI-generated machinery should not replace real machinery photography on actual sales listings.**

The website should maintain a clear distinction between:

- Marketing visuals
- Actual machinery inventory photographs

Actual inventory should remain visually truthful.

---

## 10. Machinery Presentation

The machine should be the visual focus of machinery-related pages.

Avoid unnecessary decorative elements competing with the excavator.

Machine cards should make the most important information immediately visible.

Typical hierarchy:

**Machine Photo**

**Brand + Model**

**Year**

**Hours**

**Price / Contact for Price**

**Status**

**View Machine**

Users should not need to read large amounts of text before deciding whether to inspect a machine.

---

## 11. Visual Style

The website should feel modern and premium while remaining extremely easy to use.

Visual inspiration may be taken from modern product websites such as Samsung in areas such as:

- Large product presentation
- Generous whitespace
- Layered visual composition
- Smooth scrolling
- Subtle floating effects
- Controlled animation
- Strong typography
- Product-focused storytelling
- Clean transitions

The website should not copy another company's visual identity or interface.

The objective is to adapt modern product-presentation principles to the heavy machinery industry.

---

## 12. Motion and Animation

Motion should be used to improve presentation rather than create complexity.

Animation should feel:

- Smooth
- Controlled
- Premium
- Deliberate
- Subtle

Avoid:

- Excessive animation
- Constant movement
- Unnecessary loading animations
- Complicated navigation effects
- Animation that delays access to content
- Effects that significantly reduce website performance

The website should remain understandable if all decorative animation is removed.

---

## 13. Signature Homepage Motion

The homepage may feature a real HGR excavator as the primary hero visual.

The excavator should initially appear in a natural stationary position with:

- Excavator body stationary
- Tracks stationary
- Bucket positioned on or close to the ground
- Boom and arm in a natural lowered position

As the visitor scrolls, the excavator boom and arm should gradually rise.

The machine body should remain visually anchored.

The movement should follow the scrolling direction where technically practical:

**Scroll down → arm rises**

**Scroll up → arm lowers**

At the end of the animation sequence, the excavator arm should reach a natural high position.

The animation should then transition naturally toward the machinery inventory section.

The effect should resemble real excavator movement rather than an artificial floating animation.

---

## 14. Motion Performance Rule

Website speed takes priority over decorative animation.

The signature excavator animation must be implemented efficiently.

The animation should:

- Avoid unnecessarily large video files
- Avoid excessive JavaScript
- Avoid layout shifts
- Use optimised assets
- Remain smooth on supported devices
- Have a simplified mobile implementation where appropriate
- Provide a static fallback where necessary
- Respect reduced-motion accessibility settings

If the animation causes unacceptable website performance, it should be simplified rather than sacrificing website speed.

---

## 15. User Interface Philosophy

HGR customers should never need to learn how to use the website.

Navigation should use familiar patterns.

Important actions should be visually obvious.

Primary actions include:

**View Excavators**

**View Machine**

**WhatsApp HGR**

**Call HGR**

**Send Enquiry**

Avoid creative button labels that require interpretation.

---

## 16. Primary Customer Journey

The primary website journey is:

**Arrive**

↓

**Understand that HGR sells used excavators**

↓

**View available excavators**

↓

**Choose a machine**

↓

**View machine details**

↓

**Contact Us**

Every major design decision should support this journey.

---

## 17. Homepage Priority

The homepage should not attempt to keep visitors on the homepage unnecessarily.

Its primary purpose is to direct potential machinery buyers toward available inventory.

The main machinery call-to-action should appear immediately within the hero area.

Visitors should be able to bypass animations and go directly to available excavators.

The homepage should introduce HGR sufficiently to establish trust without creating unnecessary steps before the inventory.

---

## 18. Mobile Experience

Mobile usability is a priority.

The mobile website should provide:

- Large tap targets
- Readable typography
- Simple navigation
- Fast access to inventory
- Fast access to WhatsApp
- Fast access to phone contact
- Clear machine photographs
- Easy-to-read specifications

Desktop visual effects should not automatically be reproduced on mobile if they negatively affect performance or usability.

Mobile design should favour simplicity.

---

## 19. Brand Voice

HGR's written communication should be:

- Direct
- Clear
- Helpful
- Professional
- Practical
- Easy to understand

Avoid unnecessary corporate language.

Avoid exaggerated claims such as:

- Best in Malaysia
- No.1 excavator dealer
- Cheapest excavators
- Highest quality in Malaysia

unless such claims can genuinely be substantiated.

Prefer factual language.

Example:

**Used Excavators for Sale in Malaysia**

rather than:

**Malaysia's Ultimate Destination for World-Class Heavy Machinery Solutions**

---

## 20. Product Information Philosophy

Machinery buyers should receive useful information quickly.

Prioritise:

- Model
- Year
- Hours
- Condition
- Engine
- Operating weight
- Origin
- Price
- Photos
- Video
- Availability

Descriptions should supplement these facts rather than replace them.

---

## 21. Trust

Trust should be created through evidence rather than exaggerated marketing.

Useful trust elements include:

- Real machinery photographs
- Clear pricing where available
- Detailed specifications
- Physical Klang yard
- Direct phone contact
- Direct WhatsApp contact
- Actual machine videos
- Clear company information
- Professional website presentation

---

## 22. Overall Brand Principle

The HGR website should combine:

**Modern presentation**

+

**Real machinery**

+

**Simple navigation**

+

**Direct human communication**

The website can look sophisticated without becoming complicated.

When visual sophistication conflicts with customer usability, customer usability takes priority.
