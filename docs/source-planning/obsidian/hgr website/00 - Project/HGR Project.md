# HGR Equipment Resources Website Project

## 1. Project Overview

HGR Equipment Resources is a Malaysia-based heavy machinery business specialising primarily in the sale of used excavators.

The website will serve as HGR's main digital platform for generating enquiries and sales leads throughout Malaysia.

The website must be designed around lead generation rather than e-commerce. Visitors should be encouraged to contact HGR through WhatsApp, telephone calls and enquiry/quotation forms.

The website will also serve as the primary landing destination for future Google Ads campaigns.

## 5. Primary Website Objective

The primary objective of the website is:

**Generate qualified sales enquiries for HGR Equipment Resources.**

The website is not primarily an online store.

Visitors should be encouraged to contact HGR regarding available machinery.

Primary conversion methods:

1. WhatsApp enquiry
2. Telephone call
3. Website enquiry/email

Quotations can be requested through these channels. Machinery sales, rental and service transactions normally proceed offline after enquiry; lubricant enquiries may also proceed offline in V1.

WhatsApp should be treated as an important conversion channel because customers may want to quickly ask questions about a particular machine.

## 13. SEO Direction

The website should be structured to support search visibility for relevant heavy machinery searches in Malaysia.

The primary SEO focus should initially centre around used excavators and related high-intent searches.

Secondary business areas such as excavator rental, machinery servicing, lubricants and other machinery categories can receive dedicated SEO sections as the website grows.

The SEO planning documents define approved qualitative keyword themes, page structure, metadata, internal linking and content strategy; quantitative metrics remain to be collected.

The supplied external qualitative research and human-approved strategy now prioritise /used-excavators, individual machine pages, homepage and Klang trust signals ahead of secondary Rental, Machinery Services and Lubricants. V1 SEO is inventory-led; no blog or extra model/category/location/rental SEO pages are required.

Qualitative themes are research-informed, not volume-ranked. Keyword Planner volume, CPC, Ads competition, difficulty and forecasts remain uncollected. Future expansion depends on actual search, Ads, Mudah, enquiry and inventory data.

## 18. User Experience Direction

The HGR website should be extremely simple to understand and navigate.

A significant portion of HGR's target customers may be contractors, machinery operators and business owners who are not highly tech-savvy.

The website must therefore prioritise clarity over complexity.

The primary customer journey should be:

Visitor arrives
→ Understand that HGR sells used excavators
→ View available excavators
→ Select a machine
→ View machine details
→ Contact HGR through WhatsApp, phone or enquiry form

The website should minimise the number of steps required to reach the machinery inventory.

### Primary Navigation Principle

"Used Excavators" or an equivalent inventory call-to-action should be one of the most prominent actions throughout the website.

Visitors should not need to understand HGR's website structure before finding available machines.

Important actions should use clear language rather than technical or creative labels.

Examples:

- View Excavators
- Used Excavators
- View Available Machines
- WhatsApp HGR
- Call HGR
- Contact Us

Avoid unclear labels that require interpretation.

---

## 19. Visual Experience

The desired visual direction is a modern, clean and premium website with inspiration from the visual experience of websites such as Samsung.

The objective is not to copy Samsung's website design.

Relevant visual characteristics include:

- Large high-quality imagery
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Generous spacing
- Clean layouts
- Smooth scrolling
- Subtle floating elements
- Layered visual effects
- Smooth entrance animations
- Subtle movement during scrolling
- Modern transitions
- Strong typography
- Minimal visual clutter

Animations should make the website feel modern without making it harder to use.

---

## 20. Animation Principle

Animation is secondary to usability.

Animations must never:

- Prevent immediate access to machinery
- Hide important navigation
- Require users to understand unusual interactions
- Delay WhatsApp or contact actions
- Make pages difficult to scroll
- Create excessive movement
- Significantly reduce website performance

Animations should primarily be used for:

- Gentle image movement
- Floating machinery/product imagery
- Subtle content entrance
- Smooth transitions
- Depth and layering
- Small interaction feedback

Animations should be restrained on mobile devices where necessary for performance and usability.

The website should respect reduced-motion accessibility preferences.

---

## 21. Simplicity Principle

When choosing between:

**A visually impressive but complicated interaction**

and

**A simple interaction that customers immediately understand**

HGR should choose the simple interaction.

Visual sophistication should come from photography, typography, spacing, animation and layout rather than complicated navigation.

The customer should always know:

1. Where they are
2. Where the machines are
3. How to contact HGR

## Future Product Division Expansion

The HGR website should be capable of expanding into additional machinery and product divisions without requiring the core website to be rebuilt.

Example future expansion:

HGR may become a distributor or dealer for a machinery brand, such as a forklift manufacturer.

In this situation, the website should be able to introduce a new top-level product division such as:

`/forklifts`

with individual product/model pages beneath it.

Example:

`/forklifts/[product-slug]`

New machinery products should not automatically use the same data structure as individual used excavator inventory.

Different product divisions may require different information.

Examples:

### Used Excavator

Represents one physical machine.

Potential information:

- Brand
- Model
- Year
- Hours
- Price
- Condition
- Machine status
- Individual machine photographs

### New Forklift Product

Represents a repeatable product/model.

Potential information:

- Brand
- Model
- Forklift type
- Load capacity
- Lift height
- Engine
- Fuel type
- Technical specifications
- Product brochure
- Product images
- Price / Request Quote

### Lubricant Product

Represents a consumable product.

Potential information:

- Brand
- Product name
- Lubricant type
- Grade / viscosity
- Packaging size
- Applications
- Technical documents

The technical architecture should allow new product divisions and content types to be introduced later without requiring the existing website architecture to be replaced.

[FUTURE] Selected products such as lubricants may eventually support online purchasing. The architecture should avoid blocking this expansion, but no payment gateway, commerce platform, checkout or shipping implementation is selected or required for V1.

Do not build unused future product systems during V1.

V1 should remain focused on HGR's current business requirements while preserving a practical expansion path.
