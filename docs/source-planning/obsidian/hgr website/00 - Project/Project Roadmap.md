# HGR Website Project Roadmap

## Project Goal

Build and launch a fast, professional and lead-focused website for HGR Equipment Resources at:

**hgrmachinery.com**

Primary business focus:

**Used excavator sales throughout Malaysia**

The website must support simple machinery management by the owner, strong SEO foundations, Google Ads traffic and measurable enquiries through WhatsApp, phone and forms.

Potential future improvements include:

- Bahasa Malaysia content
- Chinese content
- CRM integration

Future evidence-led scope also includes brand/model/location/service SEO pages, useful informational content, automatic marketplace integrations and advanced manual/offline sale attribution. None is automatically required for V1. Website tracking alone does not prove an offline sale came from Mudah or another source.
