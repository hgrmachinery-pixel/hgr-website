# Changelog

## Version 1.1 — 4 October 2026

Added `04-HGR-DESIGN-GUARDRAILS.md` as a permanent part of the HGR Vision documentation. The document defines principles for preserving HGR's visual identity and design philosophy across future interface work, independent of technology or contributors.

The guardrails complement the Constitution, Digital Showroom Bible, Blueprint, and Build Specification. They do not replace those documents, alter approved product scope, or authorize new features.

## Version 1.0 — 3 October 2026

**Status: Frozen.** This release establishes HGR's permanent vision baseline and its associated design and engineering references.

Created:

- `00-HGR-CONSTITUTION.md` — concise governing principles and freeze status.
- `01-HGR-DIGITAL-SHOWROOM-BIBLE.md` — structured philosophy of HGR's digital showroom.
- `02-HGR-BLUEPRINT.md` — visual and interaction reference for desktop, mobile, and key journeys.
- `03-HGR-BUILD-SPECIFICATION.md` — engineering expectations, acceptance criteria, and testing strategy.

The documents align to the approved Next.js, Payload CMS, PostgreSQL, and external object-storage architecture; the inventory-first, direct-enquiry V1; stable identities for individual machines; owner independence; truthful evidence; privacy; accessibility; performance; and portability.

This freeze records the vision and design direction. It does not mark implementation requirements as complete, approve production changes, resolve business facts still awaiting verification, or add features beyond the agreed scope. Amendments require an explicit, versioned entry here.
