# 00 — HGR Constitution

## Methodology

This project follows the [Procnias Product Method](../../docs/procnias/PROCNIAS-PRODUCT-METHOD.md). This Vision Package defines the HGR-specific implementation of that methodology.

## Purpose

HGR's digital presence exists to present real machinery accurately and help customers make direct, informed contact with HGR. It is an operating showroom and a durable record of individual machines, not an ecommerce system or a substitute for inspection and discussion.

## North Star

A customer can understand what HGR offers, find a relevant machine, assess the available evidence, and contact HGR to discuss it or arrange a viewing. HGR can keep this information accurate without routine dependence on technical staff.

## HGR Standard

Every public statement is supportable. Every machine is treated as a distinct physical asset. Important facts, status, evidence, and next steps are clear. Customer effort is respected; privacy and safety are protected; operational tools remain understandable to their owners.

## Decision Framework

Prefer, in order:

1. Truthful and useful information over persuasion.
2. The simplest maintainable approach that satisfies the need.
3. Owner control of routine operations.
4. Accessibility, performance, portability, and privacy by design.
5. Reversible, evidence-led evolution over speculative capability.

When a decision could change business claims, customer expectations, privacy boundaries, or the approved architecture, resolve it explicitly before implementation.

## Owner Independence Principle

Routine inventory and structured content work belongs in the CMS. Owners should not need source code, a terminal, or a developer for ordinary publication and updates. Technical controls should not burden normal editorial work.

## Memory Principle

Each physical machine has its own durable identity and history. Its public address is not reassigned to another machine. Material decisions, evidence, and customer-relevant history should remain intelligible over time, including after a machine is sold.

## Product Principles

- Lead with genuine available excavators and clear routes to direct contact.
- Treat the website as a lead-generation showroom; sales and viewing arrangements remain direct and offline.
- Use real, rights-approved evidence and disclose only verified facts.
- Keep machine photography structured, coherent, and useful for inspection.
- Make motion optional; information and actions remain available without it.
- Keep supporting business areas proportional to verified offerings and current need.
- Separate private originals from any reviewed, approved public derivative.
- Keep the application portable across compatible hosts and services.
- Do not add ecommerce, CRM, automatic marketplace synchronisation, or speculative catalogues without a separate decision.
