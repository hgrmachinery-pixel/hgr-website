# Roadmap: HGR Equipment Resources Website

## Overview

Deliver an English V1 used-excavator lead-generation website through eight dependent phases. First verify the approved architecture and the real Exabytes account. Then establish permanent physical-machine identity and the owner's Payload workflow, add structured photography and protected JPJ documents, and deliver the public inventory and machine pages. Complete direct enquiries and compact secondary pages, then accessibility/visual polish and inventory SEO. Finish measurement, privacy, security and recovery before owner acceptance and authorized launch readiness. Later business divisions and systems remain outside this V1 milestone.

This roadmap plans V1 work. Phase 1 has four plan summaries covering the foundation, documentation and conditional host findings; local end-to-end integration and actual host workload verification remain pending. Phase 5 implementation is complete; its live contact configuration and legal page publication remain deferred to deployment. This roadmap does not authorize paid resources, DNS changes, production deployment or publication. Later plan files will be created during each phase's planning step. No date or week estimate is promised by the historical source roadmap.

## Phases

- [ ] **Phase 1: Portable foundation and Exabytes feasibility** — establish the approved stack, environment boundaries and representative existing-account test.
- [ ] **Phase 2: Machine model and Payload owner workflow** — make every physical excavator distinct and let HGR manage drafts and core facts.
- [ ] **Phase 3: Photography, media processing and JPJ protection** — complete category-led uploads, responsive images, watermarks and human-approved public documents.
- [ ] **Phase 4: Public used-excavator inventory** — ship browse, machine detail, truthful status/price behaviour and stable public links.
- [x] **Phase 5: Enquiries and supporting pages** — implement direct contact flows and simple editable secondary sections. SMTP/live mailbox, phone/WhatsApp numbers, legal approval/publication and infrastructure verification are deferred to deployment/configuration.
- [x] **Phase 6: Accessible design and inventory SEO** — implementation complete: apply HGR design and crawlable, truthful search foundations with a static-first hero. Phase verification is human-needed only for owner-deferred browser/device and launch-asset rights review.
- [ ] **Phase 7: Measurement, privacy, security and recovery** — validate tracking, protect data and prove staging/backups.
- [ ] **Phase 8: Owner acceptance and launch readiness** — test real workflows and content, train HGR and prepare an authorized launch.

## Dependency rule

Phases execute in the listed order. Earlier phases establish security and privacy boundaries; Phase 7 audits and completes them rather than postponing access control until the end. Small preparatory tasks can happen in parallel only where they do not change the owner-approved dependencies or make unverified content public.

## V1 versus deferred scope

**V1:** The eight phases above. The core inventory, owner workflow, media security and conversion journey determine completion. Supporting pages, brand, SEO, analytics, privacy, backup and owner training are included because they make that journey usable and safe.

**Future or separately approved:** BM/Chinese, other machinery divisions, full lubricant catalogue/admin, ecommerce, CRM, customer accounts, rental booking, automatic Mudah sync, blog or bulk SEO landing-page generation, advanced comparison/filters and offline sale attribution. These do not create hidden V1 phases. See [REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md) for individual deferred items.
